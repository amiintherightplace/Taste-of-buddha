About Taste of Boudha and Sherpa Bar

A Nepalese restaurant on Baidam Road, Pokhara.

Nepalese food, Himalayan setting

Taste of Boudha and Sherpa Bar is a Nepalese restaurant in Pokhara. Its menu covers momo in several styles, noodles and thukpa, meat dishes such as sukuti and gyuma, thali sets, burgers, pizza and breakfast plates.

Drinks lean Himalayan too: herbal and medicinal teas, butter tea, fresh juices, and traditional chyang and tongba.

The room

Strings of prayer flags, embroidered wall hangings, a painted mountain mural and cushioned wooden seating give the dining room its Himalayan character. This website’s colours and mountain line follow that mural.
