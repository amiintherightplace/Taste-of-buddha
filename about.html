About us

Himalayan comfort food, teas and a bar under strings of prayer flags.

What we cook

Taste of Boudha & Sherpa Bar serves Nepali, Tibetan and Sherpa dishes: momo in six styles, thukpa and thenthuk, Sherpa meat plates and a thali with local red rice.

We also cook vegan burgers, pizza and breakfast, and pour organic juices, medicinal teas, cocktails and traditional chyang and tongba.

The room

Wooden tables, cushioned seats and a hand-painted mountain mural sit under prayer flags and woven wall hangings, with the street just outside.
