Nepalese food on Baidam Road

Taste of Boudha and Sherpa Bar serves Nepalese food in Pokhara. The menu runs from momo, chow mein and thukpa to thali sets, burgers, pizza, fresh juices and Himalayan herbal teas.

The dining room is decorated with prayer flags, wall hangings and a painted mountain mural.

About the restaurant

Chilly & Honey Momo

NPR 350

Visitors highlight the chili honey momos as a standout dish, according to the Google listing. The menu lists them with veg, chicken or buff options.

Reviewers also suggest trying the homemade chyang, which the menu lists at NPR 350.

The atmosphere

Himalayan details run through the room: strings of prayer flags, embroidered wall hangings, a mountain mural and cushioned wooden seating.
